# viral_research — decision rules

## What counts as viral
- An **outlier** has at least 3x its own channel's median views for the same format; 1.5-3x is borderline.
  Raw views never rank videos: big channels get big views on ordinary videos.
- Baselines: long-form = the channel's videos 7-180 days old, at least 10; Shorts = its 30 most recent after
  the 3 newest (the Shorts tab has no dates). Fewer than 10 → "sample too small", the channel is not scored.
- Shorts and long-form are never ranked together; neither are languages.
- Sponsored (#ad, "sponsored by", paid partnership) and live videos are not outliers.

## What counts as a pattern
- More common in outliers than in the **same channels' ordinary videos** by at least 20 points (numbers: at
  least 70% of outliers on one side of the controls' median), in at least 3 outliers across at least 2
  channels. One viral video is luck; a repeated difference is a recipe.
- Packaging (title, thumbnail, length, posting time) is reported separately from content (opening, pacing,
  structure).
- The back-test result is reported whether or not it supports the patterns.

## Use of other creators' work
- Analysis only: structure, pacing, formulas. Never reuse wording, footage, audio, or re-upload. Media stays
  in the run folder.

## Collection
- At most 4 requests at a time; everything cached in the run folder; a rerun continues where it stopped.
