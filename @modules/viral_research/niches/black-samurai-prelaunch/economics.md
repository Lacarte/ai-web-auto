## Revenue: what can and cannot be said
- **No public RPM exists for this niche** (UNAVAILABLE). Studio-verified disclosures were not found. Calculator sites are modelled estimates and contradict each other.
- **Thresholds before any ad revenue:**
  - Today YPP needs 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months.
  - **From 2027-02-01, new applicants need 8,000 hours.** A channel launched now should plan for 8,000.
  - Fan funding opens at 500 subscribers + 3,000 hours.
- **US withholding (Dominican Republic resident):** 30% of earnings from US viewers, since there is no US–DR tax treaty. Submit tax info (W-8) in AdSense; if it is missing, up to 24% of worldwide earnings can be withheld.
- **Dominican income tax:** whether YouTube income is Dominican-source (taxable under Ley 11-92 Art. 272) or foreign-source (Art. 269) is for a local accountant. It is irrelevant while revenue is zero. **No US company is needed:** an individual AdSense account in the Dominican Republic receives payment by wire, check or Hyperwallet.

## Scenarios (12 months)
- **Conservative — the outcome to plan for:**
  - No video breaks out. Typical small-channel uploads in these lanes sit at a few hundred to ~1,200 views (see the comparison).
  - Over 12 months that is far short of 8,000 watch hours, so **ad revenue is $0**.
  - Cost is ~$650–1,200 of tools plus your time.
- **Base:**
  - One or two uploads break out, as Seiiki or Zen Drift did, and the channel reaches YPP within 6–12 months.
  - Revenue = monetized views × *your* RPM ÷ 1,000, read from Studio once eligible. Nothing honest can be said about it now.
- **Optimistic:**
  - Repeat breakouts and a recognisable sound.
  - Then add lawful extras: memberships at the fan-funding tier, and streaming distribution (Creator plan or higher permits it; Spotify pays only on tracks with 1,000+ streams a year; check the distributor's AI rules).
  - **Not allowed under ElevenLabs' terms:** selling downloads, "free for streamers" music, and sample packs (music-library ban).

**Is the spend justified?** Only as a capped experiment: the pilot plan bounds it to one or two months of tools, with stop rules.
